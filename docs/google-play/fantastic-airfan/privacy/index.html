BI.H Studio · Fantastic AirFan for Android

Privacy Policy

BI.H Studio provides Fantastic AirFan, an Android fan simulator. This policy explains how the app handles information when you use it or contact us for support.

App settings and sensors

Fantastic AirFan works without an app account. It stores your fan preferences on your device, including your chosen fan style, name, appearance, sound settings and language. Device motion and proximity readings help control the simulation and its effects. The app processes these readings on your device. Its fan controls do not send the readings or saved preferences to BI.H Studio or Google AdMob.

Android backup and device transfer may include saved settings if enabled on your device. You can clear local settings through Android’s app storage settings or by uninstalling the app. Manage any saved backups separately through your device’s backup settings.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to display ads. When the advertising SDK operates, Google may receive your IP address, which can indicate a general location, device or advertising identifiers, interactions with the app or ads, and diagnostic information. Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, analyze performance, and prevent fraud. Google and its advertising partners may process advertising data according to their settings and policies. How Google uses data from partner apps.

Google documents that its Mobile Ads SDK encrypts the data it collects in transit using TLS. You can manage your advertising ID in Android settings and review the choices available through Google My Ad Center.

Support, retention, and deletion

If you email BI.H Studio, we use your email address and any information you include to respond and resolve your request. We keep support correspondence as needed to handle the request, maintain a record of its resolution, or meet applicable legal obligations. Google handles the retention and deletion of data it receives through its services under the Google Privacy Policy.

For privacy questions or requests to access or delete information held by BI.H Studio, email thebihstudio@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We will post updates on this page if the app’s data practices change.
